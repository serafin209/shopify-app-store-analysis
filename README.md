# shopify-app-store-analysis
Analyst Memo — Shopify App Store Insights

Dashboard: Shopify App Store Analysis
Reporting Period: January 2018–December 2024

Key Insight

The Shopify App Store dataset contains 500 apps and 7,980 valid reviews, with an overall average rating of 4.19. Review activity increased substantially over time, rising from 2,151 reviews in 2023 to 3,804 in 2024—an increase of approximately 77%. October 2024 recorded the highest monthly volume with 387 reviews. SEO generated the most reviews at 1,145, while Sales and Conversion combined strong volume with an above-average rating of 4.35.

Business Impact

The growth in reviews suggests increasing customer engagement and greater use of marketplace apps. However, developers replied to only about 25.5% of reviews, meaning roughly three out of every four reviews received no response. This represents a missed opportunity to resolve customer concerns, strengthen relationships, and use feedback to improve app quality.

Recommendation

Prioritize developer engagement by introducing a measurable response-rate target, starting with high-volume categories such as SEO, Reviews and Ratings, and Sales and Conversion. Leadership should monitor developer reply percentage alongside review volume and average rating each month, with an initial goal of increasing the reply rate from approximately 25.5% to at least 40%. This would help developers respond more consistently while focusing improvement efforts on the categories affecting the largest number of customers.
